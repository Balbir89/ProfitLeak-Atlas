# Recruiter / interview narrative (use only for this synthetic project)

**The business prompt:** A fictional multi-country enterprise has fragmented supplier invoicing, limited pricing oversight and no consistent process for reviewing recoverable overpayments.

**What I built:** An end-to-end analytics prototype that generates 220,000 synthetic payments, models contract terms, detects six exception patterns in SQLite, compares rules against isolated synthetic truth labels, and publishes action-focused casebooks and financial dashboards in Excel and offline HTML. The package also contains a Power BI theme, DAX and implementation plan.

**What it found in the demonstration:** About €9.84 million in *modeled* exposure, €6.88 million in modeled recoverability, and 11,890 additional approval-risk flags that have **no assigned cash loss**.

**Why the analysis is useful:** Finance teams can triage transactions by exposure, trace a finding to its payment and supplier, and experiment with realistic case recovery assumptions rather than confusing alerts with confirmed savings.

**What I would improve for real deployment:** effective-dated contracts, settlement evidence, independent human-verified labels, false-positive benchmarking, vendor-deduction rules, and a controlled case-management workflow.

**Integrity note:** Do not say "my analysis saved €6.88 million" or "I detected actual corporate fraud." This is a fully disclosed synthetic portfolio simulation.
